on a Radeon and 61 % on an A100

Turning on vLLM's own documented MTP assistant makes gemma-4-31B decode 36.9 % faster at 1K of context and 70.8 % slower at 32K. The cause is not the drafter, not the hardware and not the acceptance rate: speculation sets max_seqlen_q = 2, and one clause in an or chain reads that as "this is not decode" and drops the kernel from 128 workgroups to 8.

__CHIPS__

Abstract

vLLM documents this assistant as supported and gives a command line with no mention of context length. Measured across four depths the effect is monotonic and changes sign: +36.9 %, −14.0 %, −32.4 %, −70.8 %. By 8K it is already a net loss.

The gate is max_seqlen_q > 1 in the Triton unified-attention backend's use_3d condition. The 3D kernel splits a long KV sequence into segments and reduces them in parallel; speculation's second query row disqualifies it, and the launch grid goes from 1 × 8 × 16 to 1 × 8. The comment on that clause says the batch includes a prefill request, but the binding constraint is buffer shape: the segment buffer is allocated per sequence and indexed per token, which coincide only at one query row.

Three explanations that look better were measured and dropped. Not KV-head starvation: this model has 16 heads and is fine without speculation. Not the extra query row: calling the kernel directly, going from one row to two costs 0.08 % at 32K in one construction and 0.16 % in a second. Not the tiling constants: porting upstream's Blackwell-tuned block made it 8.7 % slower.

The claim is about a kernel, not about a vendor, so it predicts the same collapse on any CUDA machine routed onto the same kernel and its absence on a backend without the guard. A rented A100 tested both: −28.2 % at 30K and −61.1 % at 50K on the forced default, and the collapse gone on FlashInfer. Both predictions held.

That is a real cost — a rejected draft has still paid for the assistant's forward pass and the target's verification — but it cannot account for −70.8 % when the assistant is four layers wide. 2The clause

vllm/v1/attention/ops/triton_unified_attention.py
use_3d = not (
    seq_threshold_3D is None
    or ...
    or max_seqlen_q > 1          # speculation makes this 2
    or num_seqs > seq_threshold_3D
    or is_batch_invariant
)

What that clause decides is how much of the GPU the kernel is allowed to use:

The comment on the clause says the batch includes at least one prefill request. The binding constraint is different, and it matters because it says what a real fix looks like. The segment buffer is allocated as [seq_threshold_3D, num_heads_q, segments, headdim] — per sequence — and indexed as segm_output[token, head, segm_idx, :] — per token. At one query row those coincide. At two the buffer would overflow, so the guard excludes it, and max_seqlen_q > 1 catches speculation along with prefill because it cannot tell them apart.

The obvious reading is that two query rows are simply more work. Calling unified_attention directly with this model's shapes, no model and no speculation, and sweeping the number of query rows:

Two more explanations were tested and dropped. KV-head starvation: the collapse in the hybrid-SSM article comes from having 4 KV heads, but this model has 16 and is fine without speculation. Tiling: upstream carries a tuned_large_head block for head_size == 256 and max_seqlen_q > 1, gated to Blackwell, with every other condition matching this hardware; porting its BLOCK_M = 32 made 32K 8.7 % slower.

4The other vendor

A claim about a kernel makes a prediction about hardware it was not measured on. If the collapse is the 3D-to-2D drop, then any CUDA machine routed onto the same kernel should show it, deepening with context, and a backend without the guard should not. gemma-4 has heterogeneous head dimensions, so vLLM forces TRITON_ATTN whenever FA4 is unavailable — and an A100 has no FA4. That makes the prediction testable for the price of an hour of rented time.

5What the path is worth once speculation is let back in

Widening the buffers by hand and raising the cutoff recovers 32.42 tok/s at 32K against 8.85 — and speculation finally beats no speculation. That was not a fix: it cannot express "speculation's two query rows but not prefill's four thousand", and greedy output at a 30K prompt diverged from stock at token 35, with three possible causes unseparated.

vllm#45450, open since June and stalled with merge conflicts, is the shape that section asked for: the gate becomes max_seqlen_q > decode_query_len, so prefill stays excluded, and the segment buffers are sized per token.

The divergence question is settled too. A fixed-input comparison at the kernel level, 18 cases, shows each path individually bit-deterministic and the two differing by at most one bf16 ULP — at one query row, which is today's shipped behaviour, exactly as at two and four. The token-35 divergence was the pre-existing 2D/3D reduction-order reassociation that batch size already toggles in production, not a defect of the admission.

6Who reported what

The same 3D-to-2D drop was reported first, in vllm#48076 on 2026-07-09, on a single B200 running the same architecture in a different quantisation. That report's trigger is a different clause in the same or chain — num_seqs > seq_threshold_3D, a batch-size threshold — and its proposed fix derives that threshold from SM count. That fix does not reach this trigger: max_seqlen_q > 1 is independent of the threshold, so speculation keeps falling to 2D however the threshold is computed. What is new here is the second trigger, its different cause, and measurements on RDNA3.

It was posted to that thread on 2026-08-01, and on 2026-08-26 to vllm#52049, a CUDA-side report of the same symptom on the same model. That reporter confirmed that explicitly selecting FlashInfer sidesteps it, which fits: the collapse is specific to this Triton kernel and an explicit backend choice bypasses the forcing. ROCm has no FlashInfer, so on this hardware the remedy is still to gate speculation by depth.

7What is not established

The crossover

Somewhere between 1K and 8K, not measured. The same gap exists on the A100 side, where 30K and 50K were measured and where MTP stops paying on FlashInfer was bracketed rather than located.

Replication

The ROCm ladder is one process per cell. The MTP arm turns out to have a second, independent run five weeks later, and the three long depths agree to 0.45 % — but the no-speculation arm has none, and acceptance rate was not captured per cell. The A100 matrix is also one run per cell, across two VMs whose cross-session variance is unquantified.

Sampling

Everything here is temperature=0, where draft and target agree most often. Sampling should lower acceptance and make speculation worse, but that was not measured on either vendor.

One model on each side

gfx1100, TP=2, a VFIO guest without peer-to-peer, one checkpoint; and one A100, one card, one checkpoint. The mechanism is a source-level claim about a shared kernel, and the two machines agreeing is what supports it, not either measurement alone.
