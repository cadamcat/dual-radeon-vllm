RDNA3 · W4A16 · vLLM · measured on 2× RX 7900 XT

A flat 60 ms per step, under a cost
that grows with context

Twelve tokens a second was two problems

A 27B model decoded at 12 tok/s on this machine and every document here treated that as one fault with one cause. It is two costs stacked on each other: one flat, one growing with context. Separating them takes a single A/B, and once separated each turns out to have its own upstream fix, written by someone else.

__CHIPS__

Abstract

The same model family, packaged asymmetrically and symmetrically, differs by 3.241× at 1K of context, 1.786× at 8K and 1.266× at 32K. Read as a ratio that looks like one effect fading. Read as decode time per step it is the opposite: the difference stays between 58.03 and 62.79 ms, a spread of 8.2 %, while the ratio falls by 60.9 %. A flat cost sitting under a growing one.

The flat cost is the W4A16 linear kernel. An asymmetric checkpoint arrives as uint4; one line of SUPPORTED_QUANT_TYPES admits only uint4b8; the layer silently falls to Triton. Nothing warns, and the model name does not tell you which kind you have.

The growing cost is paged attention, which has its own article. Both halves have a fix upstream and neither fix is ours: vllm#40977 merged, adding a second native kernel that does accept uint4; vllm#45916 widens the split-KV attention gate and is still unmerged.

What this article contributes is the separation, and one thing found on the way: sweeping the asymmetric configuration space on gfx1100, three of twelve configurations are served by two kernels, one by Triton, and eight by nothing at all. Every asymmetric checkpoint with act-order is in the last group.

1What is being measured

Decode rate here is a differenced measurement, not a server-reported one: generate 8 tokens, generate 64, and divide the extra 56 by the extra time. That removes prefill and the first-token cost from a number that is supposed to be about steady-state decoding. Every cell runs in a fresh container off a stock image, after a warm-up generation that is discarded.

The warm-up earns its place rather than being a ritual. Every cell JIT-compiles exactly four Triton kernels, and on both arms all four land inside the warm-up, so neither timed call carries a compile. That is checked from the container logs rather than assumed, and it is checked on both arms so a compile cannot be inflating one side only.

Which kernel actually served the layers is recorded from inside the workers. Under --tensor-parallel-size 2 the linear layers are built in spawned processes, where a monkeypatch applied in the parent sees nothing at all. So choose_mp_linear_kernel is patched on disk before the engine starts and each rank writes its own verdict. Every cell in this article carries two distinct pids; a cell whose instrumentation never reached the workers would otherwise write an empty record and exit zero.

What the absolute numbers are worth This is a VFIO guest with no peer-to-peer between the cards and a cross-die PCIe 3.0 path, chosen as the least favourable topology rather than a showcase. The shapes here transfer; the levels are a floor. Cells measured on different container images are not comparable to each other in absolute terms, and Figure 3 marks where that applies.

2The claim this replaces

On 2026-08-24 this box measured two 27B models on the same day: a dense one at 44.8 tok/s and this hybrid-SSM one at 12.3, a factor of 3.64× with parameter count held constant. That was read as an architecture result, and it is the reading the repository's own documents carried.

It was confounded. Every quantized checkpoint in that campaign is symmetric except the two Qwen3.x AWQ ones, and those are exactly the two the documents single out as slow. The architecture comparison and the packaging difference coincide precisely where the conclusion was drawn. The A/B below is what separates them, and it is worth up to 3.24× on its own.

3One family, two packagings

Two checkpoints of the same model — Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration, compressed-tensors pack-quantized on both sides, same two cards, same image, one fresh container per cell. One is symmetric: false, the other symmetric: true.

Decode time per token is additive. If the cause is a per-step GEMM then it has to appear as a context-independent number of milliseconds, with the ratio falling only because the attention term grows underneath both arms. That is a prediction the ratio on its own cannot make, and it is the reason this figure defaults to milliseconds rather than to the larger-looking multiple.

4Why one of them gets a native kernel

vLLM has had a native gfx1100 W4A16 kernel since vllm#41394, registered for the same mixed-precision path compressed-tensors uses, and its own docstring says it is registered ahead of the Triton kernel for the ROCm-RDNA3 path. One line decides who reaches it:

vllm/model_executor/kernels/linear/mixed_precision/rdna3_w4a16.py
SUPPORTED_QUANT_TYPES = [scalar_types.uint4b8]

uint4b8 is symmetric int4. compressed_tensors_wNa16.py picks the weight type from the checkpoint's own symmetric flag and sets zero_points = not symmetric, so an asymmetric checkpoint arrives as uint4 and fails the type check. Asked directly, the registry says exactly that:

choose_mp_linear_kernel, recorded from inside a TP worker
no        RDNA3W4A16LinearKernel   Quant type (uint4) not supported by
                                   RDNA3 W4A16 kernel; supported: [uint4b8]
SELECTED  TritonW4A16LinearKernel

Nothing warns. The model loads, runs, answers correctly, and is slow.

Two other differences between the arms had to be retired rather than argued away. Group size, 32 against 128: no checkpoint exists that separates it from symmetry, so this A/B cannot, but can_implement has no group-size-128 condition in its source, the four corners above ignore it, and the fastest model on this box is group 32. Which layers are quantized: the two ignore lists differ, so the quantized linears were counted from each model.safetensors.index.json rather than assumed. 399 against 400, differing by exactly one layer that only the symmetric checkpoint quantizes — which gives the symmetric arm one more W4A16 GEMM, so it cannot be what makes that arm faster.

5The other term

The flat block is the whole of the difference between the arms, but it is nowhere near the whole of the decode step. At 32K both arms spend most of a step inside kernel_paged_attention_2d, whose per-call cost grows by a factor of 28 between 1K and 32K while every other kernel in the profile stays flat. That is the second problem, it is not about quantization at all, and it has its own article and its own fix.

The two terms are why neither headline number was ever a good summary. At 1K the packaging penalty is essentially the entire gap; at 32K it is a rounding error next to attention. A single ratio quoted without its context length hides which of the two it is mostly measuring.

6Two fixes, neither of them ours

vllm#40977 merged on 2026-07-14 and added RDNAHybridW4A16LinearKernel, which accepts uint4, group size 32, and all of gfx11. So on current main an asymmetric checkpoint does get a native kernel — a different one from the kernel this investigation was about, reached by widening the registry rather than the type gate. The same checkpoint that decoded at 11.41 tok/s against Triton decodes at 37.32 there.

vllm#45916 is the attention half, and this repository's contribution to it is the gfx1100 end-to-end verification rather than the code.

A cross-campaign control makes the 0.27 arm worth trusting on its own terms. The 1K stock cell was measured twice by the split-KV campaign and once more, weeks apart and by a different script, as the hybrid arm of the kernel pair in this directory. The two campaigns land 0.21 % apart. Neither was corrected towards the other.

7What is left, and why it is not a pull request

The type gate can be opened. Doing it exposes a second problem behind the first, which is the part worth reporting: the checkpoint ships its zero points as (N/8, groups) and the kernel wants (groups, N/8), so the layer selects, loads, and then refuses at the kernel entry with b_scales must have same group count as qzeros. The HIP kernel was never reached with wrong data; it declined the shapes at the door.

Three Python lines fix both halves: admit uint4, run the transpose that process_weights_after_loading already runs on the other two tensors, and pass the zero-point convention the kernel has always supported instead of hard-coding the GPTQv1 one. 3.11× on 0.23.1, with output token for token identical to the Triton control.

The control arm is the reason to believe the third line An arm with only the first two changes runs at 35.45 tok/s against 35.50, so it really is executing the native kernel. Its mean logprob is 24× worse and its output is terasterasterasteras…. A patch that stopped after the layout fix would have looked like a success on every axis except correctness.

On main it is still the wrong patch. Where Hybrid competes it wins by 5.9 % (37.32 against 35.22), and RDNA3W4A16LinearKernel sits ahead of Hybrid in the ROCm priority list, so admitting uint4 there hands the layer to the slower of two working kernels. The patch cannot tell that case apart from the case where it is the only option.

The eight are checked numerically rather than left at the registry's word: a real desc_act permutation against an fp32 reference that indexes scales and zero points through g_idx, fp16 and bf16, decode and prefill shapes. Four cases fail before the patch, four pass after, and the full suite goes to 54 passing with no regression.

That is where this sits. Not a pull request, because taking the nine without the three needs a decision that belongs to whoever owns the priority list, and the same argument was already made from the gfx1151 side on vllm#46186. Widening Hybrid instead is probably what upstream wants, and that is not our code to change.

8What is not established

That the flat term is only the GEMM

The additive decomposition is consistent with a per-step cost that does not depend on context, and the kernel record says which GEMM ran. It does not by itself exclude some other flat term riding along with it. What is measured is that the difference between the two arms is flat to 8.2 % over a 32× change in context, and that the arms differ in which linear kernel serves them.

Accuracy

These are two independent quantizations by two publishers at different group sizes, and this measures speed only. "Prefer a symmetric checkpoint on RDNA3" is a throughput statement. Whether the two are equally good models is a separate question nothing here answers.

How far the result carries

One machine, one model family, gfx1100 only, TP=2, bf16 activations only. The partition_scales and channel-wise parameter paths are untouched, and the permute assumes the group-quantized layout. The coverage sweep is a registry verdict on synthetic parameters; which of those nine configurations anyone actually ships is not measured.

One run per cell in the A/B

Six cells, one container start each. The repeat that exists covers the symmetric arm only and moves it by at most 1.31 %. The 0.27 arms in Figure 3 carry two runs each and their own ranges.

9What has changed since

2026-07-14 · the fallback stopped being Triton vllm#40977 merged, adding RDNAHybridW4A16LinearKernel. On main an asymmetric checkpoint reaches a native kernel and the "falls back to Triton" framing applies to 0.23.x and to builds without that PR. Everything about RDNA3W4A16LinearKernel itself — the type gate, the transposed zero-point layout, use_v2_format — is unchanged on main.
2026-08-27 · measured on 0.27, and the first reading was too broad The pair on a 0.27.1.dev5 image put Hybrid ahead by 5.9 %, which was first written down as "the patch is a loss". It is a loss on that configuration. The sweep the following day found three configurations where it displaces a faster kernel and nine where it is the only one, and the claim was narrowed to match.
2026-08-28 · the campaign's architecture claim was corrected in place The 3.64× between two 27B models was attributed to architecture, but is confounded with packaging. The affected statements in README.md, docs/benchmarks.md and docs/architecture-notes.md were corrected and dated, and docs/open-questions.md §9 is marked answered. What survives untouched: every slope conclusion, and the ordering among the four models that are all on their best kernel path.
Writing the tests changed the patch The first version keyed the zero-point convention on c.zero_points. That passed the end-to-end run but broke ten existing cases, because a GPTQ checkpoint stores zero points explicitly and still uses the v1 "+1" offset. The right key is not c.weight_type.has_bias(). Keying on zero_points would have silently mis-dequantized every asymmetric GPTQ checkpoint on RDNA3 while passing the run that was supposed to validate it.
2026-08-29 · one of the two fixes is merged and the other is not The abstract said "Both halves are now fixed upstream", which puts a merged change and an open pull request in one basket. vllm#40977 merged; vllm#45916 has not, and docs/benchmarks.md says so — "unmerged, so on a stock install this stands". Section 9 had it right and the abstract did not. Corrected in place.
