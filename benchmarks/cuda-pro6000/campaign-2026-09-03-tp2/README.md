# Two RTX PRO 6000 without NVLink, TP=2 — 2026-09-03

The interconnect arm. `nvidia-smi nvlink -s` is empty on this pair, both
at probe time and inside the run, so this is the same tensor-parallel
measurement as `cuda-h100/campaign-2026-09-03-tp2` over PCIe instead of
NVLink -- the direct analogue of the Radeon pair this repository is
about, on current silicon.

The collective sweep for both pairs is in `allreduce-2026-09-03`, and it
is where the interesting number is: two cards without NVLink cost 20 %
over two with it at batch 1, and **four cost 171 %**.

**5 configurations, 75 measurements, 0 errors.** Part of the rented sweep of 2026-09-03; the cross-machine reading, the two platform controls and the collective are in [`cuda-modal/README.md`](../../cuda-modal/README.md).
