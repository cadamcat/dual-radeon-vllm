# A100-80GB on Modal — the platform control — 2026-09-03

Every headline ratio in `cuda-modal/README.md` divides a rented card by an
A100 that **Colab** measured. This is the same model on the same card on
the platform the numerators came from, at Colab's `mml` 33 000 and its
eleven rungs.

**Four rungs inside 0.07 %** of Colab's 2026-08-30 sitting -- closer than
Colab's own two sittings agree with each other. The band was not chosen
after the fact: Colab measured this arm twice in August and both rows have
been committed since.

`B8` here is one rung. The run was stopped after it to free credit for a
collective sweep; its two rounds agree to 0.17 % and it is kept as the
point it is -- the first Qwen3-8B row on an A100 in this repository, which
makes the H100/A100 ratio for the most memory-bound model 1.683x.
